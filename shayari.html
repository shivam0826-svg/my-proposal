A Few Words For You ❤️

Kuch baatein sawaalon mein nahi kahi jaati…
isliye ye kuch lines tumhare liye.

tujhe dekhne ka ilzaam ab aankhon pr hi rehne de
tere chehre ki taarif lafzon se bhar behne de

teri hasi mein koi raaz hai main har roz kho jaata hun
tu yun hi chalti rahe main har mod pr ruk jaata hun

teri aawaz se jazbaton ko rag mil jaata hai
teri tasveer bhi dekhu toh din accha beet jaata hai

tu gazal bhi hai, tu kavita bhi, tu sukoon ki dawa si hai
tere bina ye shaam adhoori, teri mojoodgi hawa si hai

bas itni guzarish hai —

kabhi nazrein milein toh thehar jaana,
agar ye alfaaz pasand aaye toh chai pe baith ke
iqraar kar jaana.
